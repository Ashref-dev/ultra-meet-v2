# Ultra Transcribe design system

## Direction

Warm Instrument. Operate mode. A compact recording instrument in the menu bar and a quiet reading surface for transcripts. Native macOS chrome (toolbar, sidebar and popover materials, Liquid Glass on action controls) sits over our own warm paper palette. The brand lives in precise details: the orange accent, the dot logo, dot-matrix waveform and progress, monospaced uppercase metadata, and green You / orange Colleagues. Content (transcripts, notes, settings cards) stays on opaque paper; glass is only used on controls, never nested.

## Tokens (`Sources/Views/Theme.swift`)

- Surfaces, adaptive light / dark: `background` RGB 0.965/0.955/0.94 and 0.11/0.105/0.10; `paper` white and 0.16/0.15/0.14; `secondary` 0.43/0.39/0.35 and 0.73/0.70/0.66; `line` black 9% and white 10%. `backgroundColor` is the same background as an `NSColor`, used for window backgrounds.
- Accent: `orange` (the logo ink) for primary actions, focus and selection; `ember` when pressed; `selectionFill` is orange at 12%. The same orange is the app's accent color: `packaging/Assets.xcassets` (AccentColor), compiled into `Assets.car` by `scripts/build.sh` and named by `NSAccentColorName`, so alert and dialog buttons, focus rings and text selection are orange too (unless the person picked a fixed accent color in System Settings).
- Text tones: `orangeText`, `youText` and `colleaguesText` color small text (speaker names, talk time, orange mono labels such as Rec and Renamed by AI, text links, the selected tab), darker in light mode and lighter in dark mode, at least 4.5:1 on paper, background and the selection fill. Fills, dots, rails, icons and the waveform keep the base tones. Secondary text is never faded below `secondary`.
- Destructive: `destructive`, a warm red (0.78/0.20/0.14 light, 0.95/0.42/0.34 dark), always paired with a Trash glyph or a destructive label.
- Speakers: `you` green 0.10/0.56/0.36 and `colleagues` orange 0.91/0.45/0.07, with adaptive dark tones and deep tones for the waveform.
- Radii, continuous corners: `radius` 12 for cards and sections, `controlRadius` 8 for buttons, fields, rows, tabs, segmented controls and chips, `chipRadius` 5 for key caps and tags. No capsule buttons, tabs, segmented controls or chips. Only the switch track, avatars, dots, bars and the swipe Trash button are round.
- Sizes: `controlHeight` 30, `compactControlHeight` 26, `swipeActionSize` 32. Spacing uses 4, 8, 12, 16, 20 and 24.
- Type: SF for content. 11.5 pt captions and footnotes, 13 pt body, 14 pt transcript and notes, 18 pt card and sheet headings, 20 pt pane headings, 26 pt meeting and setup titles, with slight negative tracking. Button and tab labels are sentence case, 12.5 pt semibold (11.5 compact). `Theme.mono` (11 pt, uppercase, 0.6 tracking) is for metadata only.
- Motion: `feedback` 160 ms ease-out for hover, press and cross-fades; `selection` 240 ms snappy for sliding indicators and pane changes. Every animation is off with Reduce Motion.

## Components (`Sources/Views/Components.swift`)

- `ControlStyle` (primary orange glass, secondary clear glass, quiet borderless; `compact`, `expand`, `square`; hover, press 0.97, disabled 45%, destructive role in red). `IconButton` and `MoreMenu` (the ellipsis menu) build on it. `RecordLabel` swaps the record glyph for dot progress.
- `GlassControls` groups neighboring glass controls into one `GlassEffectContainer`. `glassSurface()` uses Liquid Glass on macOS 26 and later; macOS 15 uses a material or solid tint; Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast uses opaque paper with a stronger border.
- The menu-bar panel is a borderless `GlassPanel` (not an `NSPopover`, whose own frosted frame would cover the glass) drawn with `panelGlass()`: frosted (regular) Liquid Glass tinted by `Theme.glassTint`, white 20% in light mode and black 30% in dark mode. Text on the glass uses the system's vibrant `.primary` and `.secondary` (`onGlass`, `secondaryText()`); paper cards switch back to the warm palette. macOS 15 uses the popover material, and Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast an opaque background.
- `floatingSidebar(_:)`: library and Settings sidebars share their window's color (paper or warm background) and float as a card with a hairline and a soft shadow, inset 8 pt, with no divider.
- `thinScrollbars()`: every scroll view uses `ThinScroller`, a 3 pt warm knob with no track that widens to 6 pt under the pointer, in overlay style.
- `card()` (paper, hairline, 12 pt), `field(focused:)` (paper, hairline, orange focus border), `SearchField`, `RowStyle` (hover and orange selection fill), `PressStyle`, `TextLinkStyle` (an action inside a line of text, such as Restore or Show in Finder: semibold `orangeText`, underlined under the pointer), `TabStrip` (sliding orange fill), `Segmented` (sliding paper thumb inset 2 pt), `ToggleChip`, `SwitchKnob`, `KeyCaps`, `LinkLabel`, `MonoLabel`, `EditableTitle`, `SpeakerAvatar`, `DotProgress`, `DotWaveform`, `Shake`, `FlowLayout`. `InlineHint` (TranscriptViews) is the one inline message for errors, warnings and suggestions; `InlineHint(error:openSettings:dismiss:)` shows `AppState.error` the same way in the panel and the library, dismissible, with Open Settings when the message names a pane ("Settings → Recording", see `SettingsPane.mentioned(in:)`). `SettingsSection`, `SettingsRow`, `SettingsToggle` and `RowDivider` live in SettingsView.
- No system blue: every root gets `.tint(Theme.orange)` through `SurfaceRoot`, sheets set it again, and the AccentColor asset makes AppKit's own accent orange. Keyboard focus is visible: tabs, segmented controls, settings rows and sidebar, template and model rows show the orange focus ring with Full Keyboard Access. `.focusEffectDisabled()` stays only on text inputs, which show focus with the orange insertion point and, where they have a frame, their own orange border, and on `MoreMenu`, whose ring appeared when the panel opened.

## Performance

- The popover and every window are built once and reused. `SurfaceRoot` sets the `offscreen` environment value from a `Visibility` object (popover delegate, window occlusion). While offscreen, `DotWaveform` and the live card observe `LevelMeter.still` instead of the live meter, and `DotProgress` pauses its timeline.
- No file reads in `body`: the model-installed check and the meeting's audio listing are cached and refreshed when their inputs change. `MarkdownNotes` is `Equatable`. Library search folds the query once and reads each meeting once per render, finding the match and its snippet together. Copy builds its text only when clicked.
- Recording time ticks in `ElapsedClock` (`state.clock`), not in `AppState`, so only `ElapsedReader` views (panel timer, library recording row, meeting header and row) and the status item redraw twice a second.
- The in-progress transcript is lazy and follows the newest line only while the reader is at the bottom. Playback scrolls to the speaker turn that holds the playing line, since a lazy stack knows turns before their lines are built.
- Microphone lists (`InputDevices`) listen to Core Audio device and default-input changes only while their view is on screen.

## Surfaces

- **Logo:** `Sources/Views/LogoGlyph.swift` is the only definition: a 7×5 dot lattice with an orange waveform. `LogoMark` draws it in SwiftUI, the status item draws it as a template image, and `LogoGlyph.appIcon(size:)` draws the macOS icon ("Ember": top-lit orange plate on Apple's 1024 grid, baked shadow, flat white dots). `scripts/icon/generate.sh` renders `AppIcon` from it.
- **Menu-bar icon:** glyph only. Recording: columns follow live audio. Paused: the logo at 40%. Transcribing: the waveform fills from left to right (a travelling ripple until progress is known; with Reduce Motion the logo stays still). The tooltip names the state: recording time, "Downloading speech model", or "Transcribing" with a percentage once it is known.
- **Panel (380 pt):** a non-activating `GlassPanel`, key from the first click so its glass and orange controls show active while the call app keeps focus; header with logo, title, recording state and `MoreMenu`. The ready or live card is paper. Source pills (the You pill's help and VoiceOver name the microphone that records), live name, participants, talk time, health hints (a silent microphone offers Choose Microphone, which opens Settings → Recording), live lines and notes. Help tags show although the panel never activates the app. A 64 pt waveform, then the timer and grouped glass recorder actions. Recent meetings and the update hint below.
- **Library:** unified toolbar with the sidebar button (⌃⌘S, remembered). A 272 pt floating sidebar with logo, settings, start, search (⌘F) and rows with orange selection that press in slightly. Swiping a row reveals a 32 pt round red Trash button; it opens the confirmation dialog, full swipes do nothing, protected meetings have none. The detail is paper: status, grouped glass Copy (named for the open tab, ⌘⇧C, disabled when the tab is empty), Export (⌘⇧E) and More, editable title, date · duration, and Transcript / AI notes / My notes tabs (⌘1 to ⌘3). Shortcuts show as quiet `ShortcutHint` text beside their buttons. Errors appear as one dismissible `InlineHint` at the top of the detail, as in the panel, never as a modal alert. A meeting's notice offers Open Settings when its message names a pane or the speech model is missing, and Transcribe Again when that can help.
- **Transcript:** conversation map and talk time, speaker turns with avatar, name and a colored rail, per-line timestamps that seek, right-to-left alignment, language codes in mixed meetings, unsure lines underlined, the playing line highlighted. Right-click: Play from Here, Copy Line, Edit Line, Transcribe Again As, then Start Meeting Here, End Meeting Here and Restore Full Meeting. Trim: orange bracket handles on the conversation map, the cut parts at 30%, a time label while dragging, and one quiet line under the map (scissors, "Kept 02:10 to 42:10", Restore as a text link). VoiceOver moves a handle by a hundredth of the meeting, at least 5 s.
- **Player:** one glass bar with borderless play, speed and close controls and an orange slider.
- **AI notes:** empty state with template picker and Analyze with AI, inline failure with Open Settings, dot-progress loading, rendered notes with Re-analyze.
- **Settings:** 208 pt floating sidebar with orange selection, a pane heading, warm background and paper `SettingsSection` cards. Six panes. Recording names the microphone that records You (long names shorten in the middle), says when a chosen one isn't connected and which one records instead, warns whenever it is a Bluetooth headset, and adds an Input channel menu for devices with several inputs. Storage asks before a shorter audio retention deletes audio right away. Transcription lists every speech model as a radio row (name, maker and languages, then mono size, memory and speed) with Download or Installed and a delete button; the download's dot progress opens under its row. The language chips follow the model: several, one, or a plain list for models that detect the language. Model and template sheets use the same fields and controls.
- **Setup:** 580 pt window on the warm background, step dots in the header, cards inside the scrolling page, a divider and the footer actions. Six steps with Skip and Back. The microphone check records the chosen microphone; the Mac audio step shows a check only when the request worked, and otherwise the error with Open. Both checks write to the library's Scratch folder.
- **AI rename:** the row cross-fades its title and glows orange with "Renamed by AI" for 2.5 s.
